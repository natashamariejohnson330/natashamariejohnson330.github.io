Nice to meet you :) I am CS PhD student at Northeastern University, where I am advised by [David Smith](https://www.ccs.neu.edu/home/dasmith/) and am honored to have been named a Khoury Distinguished Scholar.  

My work combines humanistic and computational approaches to examine narrative media and culture. I am particularly fascinated by storytelling genres with derivative and repetitive formal elements such as genre fiction, fanfiction, and, AI-generated fiction, and I am curious about how technology shapes the production and reception of these genres. My humanistic inquiry is situated within critical frameworks such as reception studies, formalism, and feminist and queer studies. My computational interests involve developing and evaluating NLP and ML models for research in digital humanities and cultural analytics. 

Prior to beginning my PhD, I was a Visiting Researcher at CMU's Language Technology Institute (working with [Emma Strubell](https://strubell.github.io/)) and a Research Scientist at Graphika. I received an M.A. in Literary and Cultural Studies from the former institution. Before that, I studied Symbolic Systems at Stanford University, where I worked as a Research Assistant with the [Stanford Literary Lab](https://litlab.stanford.edu/) and completed an honors thesis under the guidance of [Mark Algee-Hewitt](http://markalgeehewitt.org/) and [Daniel Jurafsky](https://web.stanford.edu/~jurafsky/).
